About Us

Technology begins with curiosity.

We believe that small ideas can become meaningful projects through learning, experimentation and creative thinking.

Our Vision

Make technology easier to explore.

AN Technologies is a student-led technology initiative focused on learning, experimenting and developing ideas in electronics, Internet of Things, automation and digital development.


Our goal is to make technology more approachable through practical projects, creative thinking and problem-solving. We aim to grow step by step and learn from every experiment.
